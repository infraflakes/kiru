---
title: std::time
description: Wait for a duration.
---

`std::time` waits.

<details>
<summary><code>sleep</code> — wait whole seconds</summary>

```kiru
std::time::sleep(seconds: int)
```

- `seconds` — the number of whole seconds. A negative count stops the run.
- A stop during the wait ends the run.

```kiru
std::time::sleep(1);
```

</details>
